# Stage 6: Link Building & Off-Page Strategy - Speedy Keys

## Review Generation Strategy (Mental Availability - PRIORITY #1)

**Goal**: Close mental availability gap with ABC Locksmiths (450 reviews)

### Review Generation System

**Target**: 30-35 reviews/month (catch ABC in 12 months)

**Method 1: SMS Review Link** (PRIMARY)
- **When**: Send within 2 hours of job completion (while experience is fresh)
- **Message template**:
  ```
  Hi [Name], thanks for choosing Speedy Keys today! How did we do? Leave a quick review: [review-link]. Takes 30 seconds. Thanks! - [Your Name]
  ```
- **Review link priority**:
  1. Google Business Profile (highest priority for Map Pack)
  2. Checkatrade (high trust signal for trades)
  3. Trustpilot (good for website display)

**Method 2: QR Code** (SECONDARY)
- Print QR codes on:
  - Receipts/invoices
  - Business cards
  - Van signage ("Scan to review us!")
- QR code links directly to Google review page

**Method 3: Email Follow-Up** (TERTIARY)
- **When**: 24 hours after job if no SMS response
- **Email template**:
  ```
  Subject: How was your experience with Speedy Keys?

  Hi [Name],

  We helped you with [service] yesterday in [location]. We hope everything's working perfectly!

  If you have 60 seconds, we'd really appreciate a review. It helps other Essex drivers find reliable help when they need it.

  [Leave Google Review Button]
  [Leave Checkatrade Review Button]

  Thanks for choosing Speedy Keys!
  [Your Name]
  ```

**Method 4: In-Person Ask** (HIGH CONVERSION)
- **When**: Right after completing job, before leaving
- **Script**: "If you're happy with the service, I'd really appreciate a Google review - it helps other people in [town] find us when they're stuck. Here's a QR code you can scan now, or I can text you a link?"
- **Success rate**: 40-50% when asked in person vs 10-15% via SMS only [ASSUMPTION: Typical conversion rates per service industry benchmarks]

## Backlink Strategy

### Strategy 1: Local Digital PR (HIGH IMPACT)

**Tactic**: Pitch local news angles to Essex media

**Target outlets**:
- Essex Chronicle
- Colchester Gazette
- Southend Echo
- Yellow Advertiser
- Essex Live
- Local radio (BBC Essex, Heart Essex)

**Story angles**:
1. **"24/7 Locksmith Helps Family at 3am After Hospital Emergency"** (human interest + 24/7 positioning)
2. **"Why Car Keys Cost £400 at Dealerships But £180 at Locksmiths - Essex Expert Explains"** (consumer education)
3. **"Essex Driver Locked Out of Car 10 Times in One Year - Locksmith's Top Prevention Tips"** (seasonal hook, helpful tips)
4. **"The Hidden Cost of Lost Car Keys - And How a Spare Key Can Save You £300"** (money-saving angle)

**Outreach method**:
- Email local journalists with ready-to-publish story (include quotes, stats, photos)
- Offer yourself as "Essex auto locksmith expert" for future stories needing quotes
- Provide "10 Car Security Tips for Essex Drivers" as freebie content

**Expected results**: 2-4 backlinks from local news sites within 6 months (high domain authority, local relevance)

## Link Building DON'Ts

❌ **Don't buy links** (Google penalty risk)
❌ **Don't use link farms or PBNs** (Private Blog Networks)
❌ **Don't exchange links with unrelated industries** ("I'll link to you if you link to me")
❌ **Don't use automated link building tools** (spammy footprint)
❌ **Don't link to or from "bad neighborhoods"** (gambling, adult, pharma sites)

✅ **DO focus on**:
- Local, relevant links (Essex news, Essex business directories)
- High authority sites (gov.uk, .ac.uk, major news sites)
- Natural link acquisition (helpful content that gets cited)
