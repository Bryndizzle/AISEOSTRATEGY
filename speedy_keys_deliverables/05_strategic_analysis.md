## Strategic Recommendations (High-Level)

Based on this analysis, the recommended strategy is:

1. **DON'T fight ABC head-on in Colchester** (they have 450 reviews, you have 85 - you'll lose)
2. **DO dominate non-Colchester Essex** (Chelmsford, Southend, Basildon - ABC has zero presence)
3. **DO exploit ABC's weaknesses** (transparent pricing page, 24/7 emergency positioning)
4. **DO accelerate review generation** (target 200+ reviews in 6 months to close mental availability gap)
5. **DO create comparison/educational content** (high AI visibility opportunity - dealer vs locksmith)
