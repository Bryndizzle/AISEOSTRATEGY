- **Tools needed**: Hike SEO, Google Search Console, Analytics, Screaming Frog
- **Cost estimate**: £600-£1,000/month [ASSUMPTION: UK SEO specialist rates]

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

**Issue: Pages not indexing in Google**
- **Check**: robots.txt not blocking pages
- **Check**: Sitemap.xml submitted to Search Console
- **Check**: Internal links pointing to new pages
- **Fix**: Request indexing via Search Console

**Issue: Page loading slowly**
- **Check**: Image sizes (should all be <150KB)
- **Check**: PageSpeed Insights recommendations
- **Fix**: Compress images, enable caching, minimize JS

**Issue: Not ranking for target keywords after 30 days**
- **Check**: Keyword in H1, first 100 words?
- **Check**: Content depth (1,200+ words for service pages?)
- **Check**: Internal links from other pages?
- **Fix**: Optimize on-page SEO, add more content, build more internal links

**Issue: Getting traffic but no calls/conversions**
- **Check**: Is phone number click-to-call on mobile?
- **Check**: Is CTA above fold and visible?
- **Check**: Is content answering the user's question?
- **Fix**: A/B test CTAs, improve content clarity, add trust signals

**Issue: Reviews slowing down**
- **Check**: Are you asking EVERY customer?
- **Check**: Is SMS service working?
- **Fix**: Make it easier (QR code at point of service), offer small incentive (entry to monthly draw)

## Risk Management

**Risk: Content writer delays**
- **Mitigation**: Build 1-week buffer into schedule
- **Backup plan**: Have 2-3 content writers on standby

**Risk: Technical issues with website**
- **Mitigation**: Developer available for urgent fixes
- **Backup plan**: Keep staging site for testing before pushing live

**Risk: Not hitting review targets**
- **Mitigation**: Multiple ask methods (SMS, email, in-person, QR code)
- **Backup plan**: Incentivize reviews (monthly prize draw for reviewers)

**Risk: Google algorithm update**
- **Mitigation**: Follow white-hat SEO practices only
- **Backup plan**: Diversify traffic sources (GBP, citations, direct traffic)

**Risk: Competitor replicates strategy**
- **Mitigation**: Move fast (18-20 pages in 3 months)
- **Backup plan**: Review velocity advantage (30-35/month)

## Success Criteria & Go/No-Go Decision Points

**End of Month 1 - Go/No-Go**:
- ✅ **GO if**: 5 pages live, 25+ new reviews, GBP optimized
- ❌ **NO-GO if**: <3 pages live, <15 reviews, technical issues blocking launch
- **Decision**: Continue to Month 2 or extend Month 1 by 2 weeks

**End of Month 3 - Go/No-Go**:
- ✅ **GO if**: 15+ pages live, 100+ new reviews, 30+ leads/month
- ❌ **NO-GO if**: <10 pages live, <60 reviews, <20 leads/month
- **Decision**: Continue to Month 4-6 or reassess strategy

**End of Month 6 - Success Check**:
- ✅ **SUCCESS if**: 45+ leads/month (90%+ of goal), 200+ reviews
- ⚠️ **PARTIAL SUCCESS if**: 35-44 leads/month (70-88% of goal)
- ❌ **NEEDS PIVOT if**: <35 leads/month
- **Decision**: Scale up, maintain, or pivot strategy
