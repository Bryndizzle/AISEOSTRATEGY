# Your Simple Measurement Dashboard

**What this is:** The 8 metrics that matter, tracked monthly.

**Why it matters:** You'll see progress within 30 days—no waiting 9–12 months in the dark.

**Time to update:** 15–20 minutes per month

---

## How to Use This Dashboard

**Every month** (set calendar reminder for 1st of month):
1. Log into your measurement tools (listed below)
2. Record the 8 metrics in a simple spreadsheet
3. Compare to last month + targets
4. Adjust strategy if needed

**Tools you need** (all free or included in Hike SEO £99):
- Google Business Profile (reviews, Map Pack position)
- Google Analytics (traffic, conversions)
- Hike SEO (rankings, page performance)
- Your phone system or CRM (leads tracked)

## The 8 Metrics That Matter

### **Metric 1: Reviews Per Month**

**What it measures:** Are you generating enough reviews to catch ABC Locksmiths (450 reviews)?

**How to track:**
- Open Google Business Profile
- Go to "Reviews" tab
- Count reviews received this month
- Record in spreadsheet

**Target:**
- **Month 1:** 30–35 new reviews
- **Month 2:** 30–35 new reviews (cumulative: 145–155 total)
- **Month 3:** 30–35 new reviews (cumulative: 175–205 total)
- **Month 6:** 30–35 new reviews (cumulative: 265–295 total)
- **Month 12:** 30–35 new reviews (cumulative: 445–505 total = caught ABC)

**What success looks like:** Steady 30–35 reviews/month. If you're below 25/month, your review generation system needs fixing (see troubleshooting below).

**Where to find this in Hike SEO:** Reputation Management → Review Tracking

---

### **Metric 2: Map Pack Position**

**What it measures:** Where you rank in Google's "3-pack" local results for key searches.

**How to track:**
- Open Hike SEO → Local Rankings
- Check position for these searches:
  - "auto locksmith Colchester"
  - "emergency locksmith Essex"
  - "24/7 locksmith near me" (search from Chelmsford, Southend, Basildon)

**Target:**
- **Month 1:** #3–4 (up from #5)
- **Month 2:** #2–3
- **Month 3:** #1–2
- **Month 6:** #1 consistently

**What success looks like:** Steady upward movement. If you're stuck at the same position for 2+ months, you need more reviews or better GBP optimization.

**Where to find this in Hike SEO:** Local SEO → Map Pack Tracker

---

### **Metric 3: Emergency Callout Leads**

**What it measures:** The metric that matters most—actual leads (phone calls, quote requests, bookings).

**How to track:**
- Count incoming calls logged as "emergency lockout" or "key replacement"
- Use call tracking number (recommended) or manual tally
- Separate organic leads (from SEO) vs paid leads (from Google Ads)

**Target:**
- **Month 1:** 15–20 leads
- **Month 2:** 25–30 leads
- **Month 3:** 35–40 leads
- **Month 6:** 50+ leads

**What success looks like:** Steady month-over-month growth. If leads plateau, you need more content (expand keyword coverage) or better conversion rate (optimize existing pages).

**Where to find this in Hike SEO:** Not tracked in Hike—use your phone system or simple spreadsheet

### **Metric 4: Non-Colchester Leads (% of Total)**

**What it measures:** Are you successfully expanding beyond Colchester into ABC's coverage gap (Chelmsford, Southend, Basildon, etc.)?

**How to track:**
- In your leads spreadsheet (above), add column: "Location"
- Record customer's city/town
- Calculate % that are NOT Colchester

**Target:**
- **Month 1:** 30–40% (you're still strong in Colchester, but starting to expand)
- **Month 2:** 40–50%
- **Month 3:** 50–60%
- **Month 6:** 60–70% (most leads come from outside Colchester = you've dominated ABC's gap)

**What success looks like:** Steady increase in non-Colchester leads. If you're stuck at <40%, your location pages aren't ranking or need better optimization.

**Why this matters:** ABC dominates Colchester (450 reviews). You dominate everywhere else (1M+ population). This metric proves your geographic expansion is working.

### **Metric 7: Pricing Page Conversions**

**What it measures:** How many people visit your pricing page and then call/book?

**How to track:**
- Google Analytics 4 → Reports → Engagement → Pages and Screens
- Find `/auto-locksmith-pricing-essex/`
- Record: Page views, Conversion rate (calls/forms)

**Target:**
- **Page views:** 40–60/month (Month 2–3)
- **Conversion rate:** 15–20% (8–12 leads from pricing page alone)

**What success looks like:** 15–20% of pricing page visitors convert. If below 12%, your page needs optimization (clearer CTAs, better trust signals, simpler contact process).

**Where to find this in Hike SEO:** Page Performance → Individual Page Analytics

### **Metric 8: ROI (Return on Investment)**

**What it measures:** Is this strategy paying off financially?

**How to track:**
- Calculate revenue from new leads (Month's leads × average job value)
- Calculate costs (Hike SEO £99 + freelancer costs + any ads)
- ROI = Revenue ÷ Costs

**Target:**
- **Month 1:** 4–15× ROI (£1,125–£1,500 revenue ÷ £99–£299 costs)
- **Month 2:** 5–11× ROI (£1,875–£2,250 revenue ÷ £199–£499 costs)
- **Month 3:** 5–18× ROI (£2,625–£3,600 revenue ÷ £199–£499 costs)
- **Ongoing:** 5–10× ROI minimum

**What success looks like:** Consistent 5× or better ROI. If below 3×, either leads are too low (need more traffic) or costs are too high (cut unnecessary spending).

**Formula:**
```
Revenue This Month = (Emergency callout leads × £75 avg) + (Premium jobs × £200 avg)
Costs This Month = Hike SEO £99 + Freelancer £X + Ads £Y
ROI = Revenue ÷ Costs
```

**Example:**
- Month 2: 25 leads × £90 avg job value = £2,250 revenue
- Costs: £99 Hike + £200 freelancer = £299
- ROI: £2,250 ÷ £299 = **7.5× return**

## What to Do with This Data

### **If metrics are ON TARGET:**
✅ Continue current strategy
✅ Maintain content velocity (3–4 pages/month)
✅ Keep review generation system running (30–35/month)
✅ Consider scaling (add more budget, hire freelancer, expand to more locations)

### **If metrics are BELOW TARGET:**
⚠️ **Low reviews (<25/month)?**
- Fix: Improve review request timing (within 2 hours of job completion)
- Fix: Add incentive (10% discount on next service for leaving review)
- Fix: Make it easier (QR code on invoice, SMS link sent automatically)

⚠️ **Low leads (<80% of target)?**
- Fix: Create more content (expand keyword coverage)
- Fix: Improve existing page conversion rates (better CTAs, clearer pricing)
- Fix: Add Google Ads for immediate lead boost while SEO builds

⚠️ **Low traffic (<80% of target)?**
- Fix: Check rankings (are new pages ranking Page 1?)
- Fix: Improve on-page SEO (Hike SEO will flag issues)
- Fix: Build more internal links to new pages

⚠️ **Low conversion rate (<12%)?**
- Fix: Simplify contact process (one-click call button, no forms)
- Fix: Add trust signals (more reviews embedded, certifications, photos)
- Fix: Improve page speed (use Hike SEO speed test)

⚠️ **Low non-Colchester leads (<40%)?**
- Fix: Create more location pages (Chelmsford, Southend, Basildon need to rank)
- Fix: Improve GBP for non-Colchester areas (add more service area posts)
- Fix: Get reviews from non-Colchester customers (specify location in review request)

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### **"My Map Pack position isn't improving"**

**Likely causes:**
1. Not enough reviews (ABC has 450, you need to catch up)
2. GBP incomplete (missing photos, services, posts)
3. Citations inconsistent (NAP—name, address, phone—must match everywhere)

**Fixes:**
- Accelerate review generation (aim for 40/month instead of 30)
- Complete GBP 100% (use Hike SEO's GBP optimization checklist)
- Audit citations (Hike SEO → Local SEO → Citation Audit)

---

### **"My pages are ranking, but I'm not getting leads"**

**Likely causes:**
1. Low conversion rate (visitors aren't calling)
2. Attracting wrong traffic (informational searches, not commercial)
3. Mobile experience poor (80% of emergency locksmith searches are mobile)

**Fixes:**
- Add click-to-call button at top of every page (sticky on mobile)
- Test your mobile site speed (use Hike SEO speed test or Google PageSpeed Insights)
- Simplify contact process (remove forms, just phone number)
- Add urgency signals ("Average response time: 35 minutes," "Available now")

---

### **"I'm getting leads, but ROI is low"**

**Likely causes:**
1. Costs too high (overpaying for freelancer, unnecessary tools)
2. Conversion rate too low (too many calls that don't book)
3. Average job value too low (not upselling spare keys, premium services)

**Fixes:**
- Audit costs (do you need all tools/freelancers you're paying for?)
- Improve phone sales (train technicians to upsell: "While I'm here, want a spare key for £99?")
- Target premium vehicle owners (BMW/Mercedes pages attract higher-value jobs)

---

### **"I'm waiting 6+ weeks and still no Page 1 rankings"**

**Likely causes:**
1. Technical SEO issues (site speed, mobile-friendliness, indexing problems)
2. On-page SEO weak (missing title tags, headers, internal links)
3. Content too thin (<1,000 words, not comprehensive)

**Fixes:**
- Run Hike SEO technical audit (fixes 90% of issues)
- Expand content on non-ranking pages (aim for 1,500+ words)
- Add internal links from homepage + high-authority pages

## When to Pivot Strategy

**Scenario 1: Month 3, still <20 leads/month**

**Diagnosis:** SEO alone isn't delivering fast enough.

**Pivot:** Add Google Ads (£300–£500/month) for immediate leads while SEO continues building in background.

**Expected outcome:** 20 leads/month from Ads + 15–20 from SEO = 35–40 total (hit target)

---

**Scenario 2: Month 3, getting 40+ leads/month but 70% are Colchester**

**Diagnosis:** You're not dominating ABC's geographic gap.

**Pivot:** Pause Colchester-focused content. Create 5–6 more location pages (Chelmsford, Southend, Basildon, Brentwood, Harlow, Braintree).

**Expected outcome:** Non-Colchester leads increase to 50–60% by Month 6.

---

**Scenario 3: Month 6, leads plateaued at 35/month**

**Diagnosis:** You've captured low-hanging fruit. Need to expand keyword coverage or improve conversion rate.

**Pivot Option A:** Create 10 more pages (more services, more locations, more vehicle makes)

**Pivot Option B:** Focus on conversion rate optimization (A/B test headlines, add video testimonials, improve mobile UX)

**Expected outcome:** Option A grows leads to 50+/month. Option B improves leads per page (same traffic, more conversions).
