## How to Use This Plan

This plan is structured in **weekly sprints** with clear:
- ✅ **Actions** (what to do)
- 💷 **Costs** (what it requires)
- 📊 **Results** (what to expect)
- 🔔 **Decision points** (where you need to approve or choose)

#### 🔔 **DECISION POINT 3: Scale or Optimize?**

By Week 10, you'll have data on what's working. Two paths:

**Path A: Keep Scaling (Create More Pages)**
- Pro: Capture more keywords, more traffic, more leads
- Con: More ongoing content investment
- Best if: Current pages converting well (>18% call rate)

**Path B: Optimize Existing Pages (CRO Focus)**
- Pro: Maximize ROI from existing traffic, no new content cost
- Con: Slower growth (you're not expanding keyword coverage)
- Best if: Current pages getting traffic but conversion rate <18%

**Recommendation:** Path A if you have budget. Path B if you're at capacity. (You can always do both—just staggers timeline.)
