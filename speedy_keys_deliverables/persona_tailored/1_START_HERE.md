## The 5 Principles Behind This Strategy

You don't need to know the academic theory, but you *should* understand why this approach works. These are based on research by marketing scientist Byron Sharp (think of him as the "evidence-based marketing" guy—he proved what actually grows brands vs what marketers *think* grows them).

Here are the 5 principles translated into plain English:

### **1. Being Top of Mind**
When someone's car key snaps at 11pm, they don't comparison shop—they Google "emergency auto locksmith near me" and call the first credible option.

**What we're doing:** Get you into the top 3 Map Pack results + Google AI Overviews so you're the first option they see.

### **2. Being Easy to Find and Book**
If someone can't find your number, see your pricing, or book outside 9–5, they'll call the next locksmith.

**What we're doing:** 24/7 visibility, transparent pricing, click-to-call buttons, fast mobile site.

### **3. Get More Customers (Not Just Loyal Ones)**
Most businesses obsess over repeat customers. But in emergency locksmith work, people only need you once every 5–10 years. Growth = reaching *new* customers.

**What we're doing:** Target non-Colchester areas (Chelmsford, Southend, Basildon) where ABC has zero presence.

### **4. Being Recognizable**
What makes people choose *you* instead of "Generic Locksmith #7"?

**What we're doing:** Build a distinctive reputation for **transparent pricing** (your competitor's #1 weakness) and **24/7 availability**.

### **5. Being There When They Need You**
People search for locksmiths in specific moments: locked out of car, lost car key, need spare key, moving house, bought used car.

**What we're doing:** Create content for each scenario so you appear in Google/ChatGPT/Perplexity when they search.
