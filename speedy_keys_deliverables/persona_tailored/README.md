## Decision Guide: Which Investment Level?

Use this to choose your 90-day budget:

### **Minimum (£297 total = £99/month Hike SEO only)**
**Best if:**
- You have 10–15 hours/month to DIY content
- Budget is very tight
- You're comfortable writing your own content

**Expected results:** 60–75 leads over 90 days, £4,500–£5,625 revenue, **15–19× ROI**

**Tradeoff:** Slower (takes 2–3 weeks to create 3–4 pages instead of 1 week)

---

### **Moderate (£797 total = £99/month Hike + £100–200/month freelancer)**
**Best if:**
- You have 5–8 hours/month to oversee (not create) content
- You want faster execution without full DIY
- You value your time and want to focus on business operations

**Expected results:** 75–90 leads over 90 days, £5,625–£6,750 revenue, **7–8× ROI**

**Tradeoff:** Moderate cost, but frees your time for higher-value work (sales, operations)

---

### **Accelerated (£1,392 total = £99/month Hike + £200–300/month freelancer + £99/month Checkatrade)**
**Best if:**
- You want fastest results (hit 50 leads/month by Month 3 instead of Month 6)
- You have budget but limited time
- Checkatrade credibility matters for locksmith industry

**Expected results:** 85–100 leads over 90 days, £6,375–£7,500 revenue, **5–5.4× ROI**

**Tradeoff:** Higher cost, but maximum velocity + trust signals (Checkatrade badge)

---

### **Maximum (£2,292 total = above + £300/month Google Ads)**
**Best if:**
- You need immediate leads NOW (can't wait 2–3 weeks for SEO to kick in)
- You have budget and want to dominate market quickly
- You plan to reinvest ad revenue into more content

**Expected results:** 110–130 leads over 90 days, £8,250–£9,750 revenue, **3.6–4.3× ROI**

**Tradeoff:** Ads deliver immediate leads but stop when budget stops. SEO compounds over time. Ideal = Ads for Month 1–3 while SEO builds, then reduce/eliminate Ads by Month 6 when organic leads hit 50/month.
