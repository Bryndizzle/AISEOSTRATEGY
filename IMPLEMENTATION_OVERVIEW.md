- Strategy based on proven SEO principles

### **How involved do I need to be?**
**Minimal involvement:** Provide access to website, approve content before publishing, 30-min monthly calls.
**Optional involvement:** Weekly check-ins, content review, strategy discussions.

### **What if my industry is really niche?**
That's actually easier! Less competition, clearer audience, faster results. We specialize in small-to-medium businesses in specific niches.

Yes! We can coordinate with your developer for technical implementations, or work directly in your CMS if you prefer.

SEO takes 4-6 months minimum. If after 6 months you're not seeing improvement in rankings/traffic, we'll:
1. Audit what's not working
2. Adjust strategy based on data
3. Recommend next steps

We're data-driven and transparent. If something's not working, we pivot.
