- **Decision timeframe**: 3-7 days research

**Segment 3: Fleet/Business Accounts (10% of business)**
- **Demographics**: Fleet managers, garage owners, car dealers
- **Problem state**: Need reliable partner for recurring key services
- **Decision timeframe**: 2-4 weeks procurement process
- **Key concerns**: Pricing, reliability, invoicing, turnaround time
- **Search behaviour**: "fleet key management," "trade locksmith services"

### 3.2 Customer Pain Points (Reddit Research - r/CarTalk)

**Financial pain points**:
1. "Can't afford £700 dealership quote" - price shock/desperation
2. "Dealership wanted £650, locksmith did it for £230" - dealership gouging perception
3. "They'll charge you £500 for a key that really cost £40" - feeling ripped off

**Trust/legitimacy pain points**:
4. "Didn't find any reliable alternatives" - lack of trustworthy options
5. "Dealers are basically the only places where you can legitimately do it" - monopoly myth
6. "This is just how stealerships work" - deep dealership distrust

**Technical complexity pain points**:
7. "You need a master key to program new keys" - technical confusion
8. "Having NO working keys means replacing the immobilizer at £1000+" - catastrophic cost fear
9. "Every manufacturer has a different system" - information overload

**Emotional pain points**:
10. Helplessness, financial panic, frustration with system, fear of being taken advantage of

### 3.3 Jobs to be Done

**Functional jobs**:
- Get into/start my car (immediate - stranded)
- Get replacement key cut and programmed (same-day)
- Get spare key before emergency happens (prevent future crisis)

**Emotional jobs**:
- Escape financial panic (avoid £700 dealership quote)
- Avoid being "ripped off" (fair, transparent pricing)
- Regain control (mobile service, fast response)
- Feel like responsible car owner (get spare key proactively)

**Social jobs**:
- Not look foolish/careless (lost keys = embarrassing)
- Not be a "sucker" who pays dealership prices
- Be a responsible car owner (have spare keys)

### 3.4 Key Objections

1. **"Dealerships are the only legitimate option"** - monopoly myth
2. **"£300-400 is still gouging"** - even mid-range pricing seen as excessive
3. **"Can locksmiths program modern transponder keys?"** - technical capability doubt
4. **"Will the key actually work?"** - quality/reliability concerns
5. **"Are they licensed/qualified?"** - legitimacy concerns

### 3.5 Decision Criteria (Reddit-Validated)

1. **Price comparison to dealership** - "half the cost" is compelling threshold
2. **Can they handle transponder programming** - not just cutting, full service
3. **Legitimacy/credentials** - proper equipment, qualified, established business
4. **Recommendations from others** - reviews, forum mentions, social proof
5. **Time/convenience** - mobile service valued, fast response time
6. **Whether they have existing key or not** - "lost only key" = different problem scope
