# Example: Intake Form (Completed)

## Essential Information

### 1. Project Context
**What are you trying to achieve?**

```
Launch a content marketing strategy for a B2B SaaS product targeting finance teams
```

**Who is the target audience?**

```
Finance directors and CFOs at mid-sized companies (50-500 employees) in UK and Europe
```

### 2. Constraints
**What are the key limitations?**

```
- Budget: £15k for content creation
- Timeline: 3 months to first publication
- Resources: 1 content writer, no video production capability
```

**What must you avoid?**

```
- No hard-sell tactics
- Avoid technical jargon that assumes accounting knowledge
- Can't make specific ROI claims without data
```

### 3. Success Metrics
**How will you know this worked?**

```
- 500 downloads of lead magnet in first 3 months
- 50 qualified demo requests from content
- Average engagement time >3 minutes on articles
```

### 4. Context
**What's already been done?**

```
- Company blog exists but rarely updated (last post 6 months ago)
- Email list of ~2,000 subscribers
- Strong product but minimal brand awareness
```

**What format do you need the output in?**

```
Content calendar, article templates, and distribution strategy as separate markdown documents
```

---

## Optional: Additional Context

**Any other relevant information?**

```
Main competitor publishes weekly. We need to differentiate on quality and insight rather than volume.
CEO wants thought leadership angle but we're realistic about our current position.
```
