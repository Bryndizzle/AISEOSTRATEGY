## 🔀 Workflow Features

### Conditional Branching
The system adapts based on business type:

- **Local Businesses**:
  - Executes Prompt 3 (Local SEO & GBP)
  - Emphasizes map pack, reviews, local schema
  - Delivers GBP optimization checklist

- **E-commerce**:
  - Executes E-commerce IA Audit
  - Emphasizes category/product pages, faceted navigation
  - Delivers IA recommendations

- **Lead Gen / B2B**:
  - Executes CRO Analysis (if goal = leads/revenue)
  - Emphasizes conversion funnel optimization
  - Delivers A/B testing roadmap

### Quality Gates
Built-in validation at each stage:
- ✅ After Stage 1: ICP defined, competitors mapped, customer language captured
- ✅ After Stage 3: Content gaps identified, keyword gaps quantified
- ✅ After Stage 5: Strategy assembled, copy examples specific (not generic)
- ✅ After Stage 7: QA score ≥10/12 required to proceed
- ✅ After Stage 8: All pages have action specs, total hours calculated

### Data Integration
Systematic use of multiple data sources:
- **HikeSEO**: GSC data (primary), keyword tracking, AI visibility
- **Ahrefs**: DR, backlinks, content/keyword gaps, traffic estimates
- **Reddit**: Customer pain points, verbatim language
- **LinkedIn**: Company research, ICP content patterns
- **Google Business Profile**: Local SEO data (if applicable)
