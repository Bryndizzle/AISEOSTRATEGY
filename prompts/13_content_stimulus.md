# Prompt 13: Content Stimulus - Idea to Multi-Channel Activation

## Purpose

Transform a single content idea into a comprehensive activation package: expanded content angles, ICP-aligned messaging, and ready-to-publish copy across multiple social channels. This prompt bridges the gap between "having an idea" and "having a content calendar."

## Inputs Required

- **Content Idea**: The core concept, theme, insight, or topic to expand
- **ICPs** (Ideal Customer Profiles): Who the content is for (minimum 1, recommended 2-3)
- **Target Channels**: Which platforms to create copy for
- **Brand Voice**: Tone, style, and any restrictions
- **Goal**: What success looks like (engagement, traffic, leads, awareness)
- *Optional*: Existing content to repurpose, keyword targets, campaign context

## Output Requirements

## Quality Standards

All Content Stimulus output must be:

- **ICP-Specific**: Every piece clearly serves at least one ICP
- **Platform-Native**: Follows channel best practices and constraints
- **Hook-Strong**: Opening lines designed to stop the scroll
- **Value-Dense**: No fluff, every sentence earns its place
- **Action-Oriented**: Clear next steps for audience
- **Consistent**: Brand voice maintained across channels
- **Varied**: Mix of formats, tones, and approaches
- **Measurable**: Goals defined for each piece

---

## Success Criteria

This prompt is complete when you have:

- [ ] Core idea validated with insight articulated
- [ ] 8-15 content angles across 5 categories (core, contrarian, practical, story, trend)
- [ ] Each ICP deeply mapped (pain points, language, channel preferences)
- [ ] Channel strategy matrix showing angle-to-platform mapping
- [ ] Ready-to-publish copy for minimum 3 channels
- [ ] Each piece of copy includes 2-3 hook variations
- [ ] CTAs appropriate to funnel stage and ICP awareness level
- [ ] Content sequencing with 2-week minimum calendar
- [ ] Repurposing pathways documented
- [ ] All assumptions clearly labelled
