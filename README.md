# AI SEO Strategy Agent — Agent Workflow Builder

A comprehensive AI SEO strategy system that consolidates 12+ specialist prompts into a single, sequential execution flow. Delivers 25-30 page strategy documents with 30/60/90-day roadmaps, copy examples, and granular implementation tasks.

## Purpose

This system transforms multiple SEO research and strategy prompts into ONE reusable agent workflow that produces:
- **Complete AI SEO Strategy Document** (25-30 pages, 10 sections)
- **30/60/90-Day Roadmap** + 12-month strategic plan
- **Copy Examples Library** (50+ FAQs, 50-100 blog titles, page titles/metas)
- **Implementation Task Breakdown** (100-200 granular tasks with page-level specs)
- **QA-Validated Deliverables** (12-criteria quality assurance)

## What This System Does

Executes **12+ specialized SEO prompts** across 9 workflow stages:
1. Business fundamentals & revenue model analysis
2. LinkedIn company research (ICP, team, content activity)
3. Local SEO & GBP audit (conditional - if local business)
4. HikeSEO competitive analysis (perceived vs actual SEO competitors)
5. Traffic & analytics (HikeSEO/GSC data)
6. Ahrefs content gap analysis
7. Reddit research (customer pain points, verbatim language)
8. Full AI + SEO strategy document (10 sections, 4-layer model)
9. Jobs to be Done + keyword mapping (100-200 keywords)
10. Copy examples & content titles (50+ FAQs, 50-100 blog titles)
11. QA validation (12-criteria quality check)
12. Implementation task breakdown (page-level action specs)

**Plus conditional prompts**:
- E-commerce IA audit (if e-commerce)
- CRO & conversion funnel analysis (if goal = leads/revenue)

## Philosophy

- **Data-driven**: Base recommendations on Ahrefs, HikeSEO, Reddit, LinkedIn data (not assumptions)
- **Specific & actionable**: Every recommendation includes WHAT and HOW (no vague "improve SEO")
- **Customer voice**: Use verbatim language from Reddit research
- **Commercial context**: Align with business model, deal size, sales cycle, revenue goals
- **UK English**: Optimise, organisation, colour (configurable)
- **Assumption transparency**: All assumptions labeled with `[ASSUMPTION: ...]`
- **Challenge-focused**: Strategy must solve stated business challenge (QA validated)
