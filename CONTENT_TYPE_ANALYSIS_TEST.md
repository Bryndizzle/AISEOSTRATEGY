## How I Make These Decisions

### **Step 1: Analyze Search Intent**

I look at the KEYWORD itself:
- **Transactional words**: "get", "buy", "service", "near me", "emergency" → Service Page
- **Question words**: "can", "should", "why", "how to", "what is" → Guide/FAQ/Blog
- **Comparison words**: "vs", "or", "better", "best", "compare" → Comparison Page
- **Research words**: "data", "statistics", "trends", "evolution" → Deep Research

### **Step 2: Assess Customer Journey Stage**

- **Top-funnel** (awareness): Blog, Deep Research, Glossary
- **Middle-funnel** (consideration): Guide, Comparison, FAQ Hub
- **Bottom-funnel** (decision): Service Page, Location Page

### **Step 3: Check Competitor Content**

What's currently ranking in top 3?
- If top 3 are all service pages → Serve transactional intent with service page
- If top 3 are guides/blogs → Serve informational intent with similar format
- If there's a MIX → Opportunity to differentiate

### **Step 4: Consider AI Visibility**

- **Question-format keywords**: FAQ Hub (high ChatGPT citation potential)
- **Comparison keywords**: Comparison Page (high Perplexity citation potential)
- **Process keywords**: HowTo Guide (HowTo schema)
- **Data/research keywords**: Deep Research (citation magnet)

### **Step 5: Evaluate Business Goals**

- **High conversion value**: Service Page or Comparison Page (with strong CTA)
- **Brand authority**: Deep Research, Thought Leadership
- **Traffic/awareness**: Blog Posts, Guides
- **AI visibility**: FAQ Hubs, Structured Guides
