## Executive Summary

Speedy Keys is a 24-year-old mobile auto locksmith (#2 in Google Map Pack, 4.9★ rating) facing a **critical visibility crisis**: strong local reputation but catastrophic organic underperformance (0.47% CTR, 159 clicks/month despite 33,863 impressions).

**Core strategic insight**: Fix CTR (immediate 3-5x traffic) + exploit competitor content gaps (10-20x traffic potential over 6 months) to reach goal of 50 emergency callout leads/month.

**Competitive advantage**: Higher review rating + #2 Map Pack position + 24/7 availability. **Competitive gap**: Zero content pages (vs competitor 472 pages), zero AI visibility (vs competitor 327 citations), weak backlinks (14 vs 771 domains).
