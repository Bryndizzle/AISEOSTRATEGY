## Risk Mitigation

### **If Behind Schedule**:
1. **Prioritize ruthlessly**: Focus only on 🔴 CRITICAL pages (Homepage, Spare Keys, Dealer vs Locksmith)
2. **Reduce word count**: Aim for 1,000-1,500 words instead of 2,000-2,500 (still valuable, less time)
3. **Use AI writing tools**: Draft initial content with AI, human review/edit (saves 40-50% time)
4. **Extend timeline**: Move from 90-day to 120-day roadmap (same outcome, less weekly pressure)

### **If Budget Constrained**:
1. **DIY GBP optimization**: Business owner does GBP posts, review responses (saves 2-3 hours/week)
2. **Reduce link building**: Focus only on free directories, skip paid listings (saves £200-500/month)
3. **Phase make/model pages**: Do top 5 premium makes only (BMW, Mercedes, Audi, Ford, VW), skip others until Month 4-6

### **If Technical Issues**:
1. **Hire WordPress specialist**: One-time 8-12 hours for schema markup, technical setup (~£400-600)
2. **Use plugins**: Schema Pro (£79), Rank Math Pro (£59/year) - automated schema markup
3. **Simplify**: If schema too complex, focus on basic LocalBusiness schema only (better than nothing)
