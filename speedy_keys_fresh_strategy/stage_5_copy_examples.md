### **PRICING & COST FAQs**

**Q1: How much does car key replacement cost?**
A: Car key replacement costs depend on whether you have a spare key or lost your only key. **Spare key** (when you have a working key): £99-£150. **Lost key** (only key lost): £250-£400. **Dealer equivalent**: £350-£700. We're typically 40-60% cheaper than dealerships because we're mobile and have lower overhead costs.

**Q2: How much does a spare car key cost?**
A: A spare car key costs £99-£150 for most makes and models when you already have a working key. This includes cutting the key blank and programming it to your car's immobiliser. Premium makes (BMW, Mercedes, Audi) cost £150-£180. Dealers charge £220-£450 for the same service.

**Q3: Is a locksmith cheaper than a dealer for car keys?**
A: Yes, locksmiths are typically 40-60% cheaper than dealers. For example: Ford Focus spare key costs £99 from us vs £220 at dealer (£121 saving). BMW 3 Series spare key costs £150 from us vs £520 at dealer (£370 saving). The quality is identical - we use the same diagnostic equipment as dealers.

**Q4: Why are dealer car keys so expensive?**
A: Dealer car keys are expensive due to three main factors: **1) High overhead costs** - franchise fees, showroom expenses, multiple staff layers. **2) Parts markup** - dealers mark up key parts by 200-400%. A key blank that costs £40 wholesale gets sold for £180-£250. **3) Captive market** - many customers don't realize locksmiths can do the same work cheaper. Locksmiths have lower overhead and competitive pricing.

**Q5: Are there hidden fees for car key replacement?**
A: At Speedy Keys, no. Our quotes are all-inclusive: call-out, parts, programming, and testing. Watch out for other providers who advertise "from £X" but then add: call-out fees (£40-£80), programming fees (£50-£120), "emergency" surcharges (£30-£60), or VAT on top. Always ask: "Is that the total price including everything?" before booking.

A: Yes, locksmith keys work identically to dealer keys. We use **OEM (original equipment manufacturer) key blanks** or quality aftermarket equivalents approved for your car make. The programming is identical - same equipment, same process, same security protocols. Your car's immobiliser can't tell the difference. Both come with 12-month warranties typically.

**Q23: Can you make keys for all car makes and models?**
A: Yes, we work on all makes and models including: **German**: BMW, Mercedes, Audi, VW, Porsche. **French**: Peugeot, Renault, Citroen. **British**: Land Rover, Jaguar, Mini. **Japanese**: Toyota, Honda, Nissan, Mazda, Lexus. **Korean**: Kia, Hyundai. **American**: Ford, Vauxhall (GM). **Italian**: Fiat, Alfa Romeo. We have the diagnostic equipment for modern keyless systems including BMW Comfort Access, Mercedes Keyless Go, Audi Advanced Key, and others.

**Q25: Can you cut keys from the VIN if I've lost all keys?**
A: Yes, when you've lost all your keys, we can create new keys from your **VIN (Vehicle Identification Number)**. We access the key code database using your VIN, cut a new key blank to the correct code, then program it to your immobiliser. This takes 60-90 minutes. We need your V5 logbook and photo ID to prove ownership before we can do this.

**Q26: What if my car has an immobiliser?**
A: All modern cars (post-1998) have immobilisers, and we work with them daily. Immobilisers prevent engines starting unless the correct key chip is detected. When we create a key, we program the transponder chip so your immobiliser recognizes it. This is standard in all our key replacements - your new key will start your car just like the original.

**Q27: Can you fix a key that's stuck in the ignition?**
A: Yes, we provide ignition extraction and repair services. Common causes: **Worn ignition barrel**, **Damaged key blade**, **Immobiliser fault**, **Steering lock engaged**. We diagnose the issue, extract the key safely (non-destructive), repair or replace the ignition if needed, and cut/program new keys if yours is damaged. Pricing: £80-£200 depending on whether repair or replacement is needed.

**Q28: Do you work on vans and commercial vehicles?**
A: Yes, we work on all vans and commercial vehicles including: Ford Transit, VW Transporter, Mercedes Sprinter, Vauxhall Vivaro, Peugeot Boxer, Citroen Relay, Renault Trafic, Nissan NV200/NV400. Commercial keys often cost slightly more due to larger blanks and complex immobiliser systems: £120-£280 typically vs £99-£180 for cars.

**Q29: Can you reprogram my existing key if it's stopped working?**
A: Sometimes. If your key has **lost programming** (stops starting car but still locks doors), we can often reprogram it for £69-£89. If the **transponder chip is damaged**, we'll need to replace the key. If the **battery is dead** (remote not working but car starts), you can replace the battery yourself (£3-£5 from Timpson/Amazon). We diagnose the issue when we arrive.

**Q30: Will using a locksmith void my car warranty?**
A: No, using a locksmith does not void your car warranty. Under **UK consumer protection laws** and **Block Exemption Regulation**, you're entitled to use independent specialists for servicing and repairs without voiding warranty. Locksmiths don't modify your car's systems - we use manufacturer-approved equipment to program keys. Warranty only applies to the car, not the key.
