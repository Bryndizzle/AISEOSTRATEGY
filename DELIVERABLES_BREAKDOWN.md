## **Links (Link Building)**

### **What is a "link"?**

A quality backlink from another website to yours. Each link includes:
- ✅ Outreach to relevant website (from your strategy)
- ✅ Relationship building with site owner
- ✅ Content placement (guest post, resource link, citation)
- ✅ Natural anchor text (not spammy)
- ✅ Contextual link (in body of content, not footer/sidebar)
- ✅ Relevant website (industry, local, or authority site)
- ✅ Link tracking and reporting

### **Types of links we build:**

**Local Citations**
- Local business directories (Yell, Yelp, Thomson Local)

- Community websites

**Authority Links**

## **Google Business Profile (GBP) Posts**

### **What is a "GBP post"?**

A post that appears on your Google Business Profile (the box that shows up when people Google your business). Each post includes:
- ✅ 100-300 words of content
- ✅ Eye-catching image
- ✅ CTA button (Call, Book, Learn More)
- ✅ Optimized for local search
- ✅ Published directly to your GBP

### **Types of GBP posts:**

**Offers**
- "10% Off Emergency Callouts This Month"
- "Free Boiler Health Check with Any Repair"

**Updates**
- "Now Offering Same-Day Emergency Service"
- "Extended Hours for Winter - Open 24/7"

**Events** (if applicable)
- "Boiler Maintenance Workshop - Free for Homeowners"

**Products/Services**
- "New: Smart Thermostat Installation"
- "Burst Pipe Repair - 2 Hour Response Time"

**Seasonal Tips**
- "Winter Boiler Tips - Keep Your Home Warm"
- "Preparing Your Plumbing for Summer Holidays"

### **Example Deliverable:**

**Package: 4 GBP posts/month**

You get:
- Week 1: Offer post ("10% off emergency callouts")
- Week 2: Seasonal tip post ("5 signs your boiler needs servicing before winter")
- Week 3: Service highlight post ("Same-day emergency plumber - available 24/7")
- Week 4: Customer testimonial post (social proof)

**Note:** GBP posts expire after 7 days, so weekly posting keeps your profile fresh.

## **Other Deliverables Included**

### **Technical SEO Fixes**

Includes:
- ✅ Fix broken links (404 errors)
- ✅ Improve site speed (image compression, caching, minification)
- ✅ Mobile optimization issues
- ✅ XML sitemap creation/submission
- ✅ Robots.txt optimization
- ✅ Fix crawl errors (from Google Search Console)
- ✅ HTTPS security check
- ✅ Canonical tag issues
- ✅ Duplicate content fixes

### **Schema Markup**

Structured data that helps search engines (and AI) understand your site:
- ✅ LocalBusiness Schema (name, address, phone, hours)
- ✅ Service Schema (services offered)
- ✅ Review Schema (star ratings in search results)
- ✅ FAQ Schema (FAQs appear in Google search)
- ✅ Breadcrumb Schema (navigation in search results)

### **Internal Linking**

Strategic links between your pages:
- ✅ Link service pages to relevant blog posts
- ✅ Link blog posts to service pages (conversions)
- ✅ Link location pages to main service pages
- ✅ Link FAQ page to relevant services
- ✅ Create content "hubs" (pillar content strategy)

### **Content Calendar**

3-6 month plan showing:
- ✅ Which blog topics to publish (and when)
- ✅ Which pages to create (priority order)
- ✅ Seasonal content opportunities
- ✅ Link building targets each month
- ✅ GBP post schedule

### **Copy Examples**

Templates for your team:
- ✅ 20+ title tag examples
- ✅ 20+ meta description examples
- ✅ Email templates for link outreach
- ✅ FAQ question/answer examples
- ✅ CTA variations
