## Example Completed Brief

**The idea**: Most businesses waste 80% of their content because they publish and forget

**ICP 1**:
- Who: Head of Marketing at B2B SaaS (50-200 employees)
- Their world: Stretched thin, owns content but can't do it all, pressure to show ROI
- Their struggle: Creates content but doesn't know if it's working, no time to optimise

**ICP 2**:
- Who: Founder doing their own marketing
- Their world: Wearing 10 hats, knows marketing matters but it's not their zone of genius
- Their struggle: Spends time on content, sees no results, feels like shouting into void

**Channels**: LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Email

**Brand voice**: Direct, slightly provocative, expertise-driven

**Goal**: Build thought leadership, drive newsletter signups

**Contrarian angle**: The problem isn't creating more content—it's that you're not extracting value from what you already have

**Timing**: Evergreen, but strong Q1 angle (new year planning)
