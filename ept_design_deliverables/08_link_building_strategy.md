## 2. Why Backlinks Matter Differently for Professional Services

### Entity Confirmation vs. Raw Volume

For a product or e-commerce site, link volume drives rankings. For a professional services firm — especially one in a licensed discipline like landscape architecture — links serve a different primary function: **entity confirmation**.

An entity is a named thing (person, organisation, place) that search engines and AI systems have a confident, consistent representation of. When Google or ChatGPT is asked "who are the leading landscape architecture firms in Southern California," they consult their entity knowledge graphs. Firms with well-established entity signals appear. Firms without them do not — regardless of how good their work is.

Entity confirmation links are:
- Links from the websites of named project clients (UCLA, Cedars-Sinai, City of Glendale)
- Links from professional association directories (ASLA, AIA)
- Links from named collaborators (HOK, Gensler, SOM, Perkins+Will)
- Links from media outlets that covered a named project
- Links from government or institutional project pages that list design team members

These links tell AI systems: "EPTDESIGN is a real, legitimate, professionally-credentialed firm that works with these institutions." That is the foundation of AI citation eligibility.

### The AI Citation Connection

AI systems such as ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity build their understanding of entities from the open web. A firm that appears on:
- Its own website
- ASLA's member directory
- Three university project pages
- Two news articles
- One architecture publication feature

...is a credible entity. A firm that appears only on its own website is not. EPTDESIGN is currently closer to the latter. This strategy moves it toward the former.

EPTDESIGN has already done the work. The projects exist. The clients exist. ## 6. DR Progression Target: 29 → 40 Within 12 Months

### How DR Works

Domain Rating is a logarithmic scale. Moving from DR 10 to DR 20 requires far fewer links than moving from DR 60 to DR 70. EPTDESIGN's current DR of 29 means that a cluster of high-DR backlinks will produce a meaningful and visible DR increase.

### Projected DR Trajectory

| Timeframe | Referring Domains (new) | Expected DR | Key Drivers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Month 0 (baseline) | 22 | 29 | — |
| Month 3 | +20-28 (total 42-50) | 32-35 | University + government project pages; directories; HOK/Gensler citations |
| Month 6 | +10-15 more (total 52-65) | 35-38 | Editorial coverage; healthcare citations; ASLA/AIA enhancements |
| Month 9 | +8-12 more (total 60-77) | 37-40 | Sustained thought leadership; speaking bio pages; LA Magazine feature |
| Month 12 | +5-8 more (total 65-85) | 39-42 | Compounding effect of earlier links; Phase 3 Riverwalk media coverage |

> **Assumption:** DR projection is based on acquiring several high-DR links (DR 65+ from university .edu domains or major architecture publications) within the first 90 days. If university and government outreach is slow to convert, DR movement may lag by 1-2 months. DR 40 is achievable within 12 months with consistent execution; DR 45 is achievable within 18 months.

### Why DR 40 Matters

At DR 40, EPTDESIGN enters the range where:
1. Google treats the domain as an established authority for its core topics
2. AI citation systems have enough cross-referenced signals to include EPT in topical answers
3. Individual project pages can rank for competitive local and topical search terms without needing exceptional content volume
4. The firm becomes visible to prospective clients using AI tools to identify qualified consultants

DR 40 is not a finish line — it is the threshold at which organic growth begins to compound. Above DR 40, content investments produce significantly higher returns than below it.
