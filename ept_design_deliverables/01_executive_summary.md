## The Business Case: What Does Invisibility Cost a 64-Year Firm?

EPTDESIGN has been shaping the built landscape of Southern California since 1962. You have designed estates for high-net-worth clients whose names you will not find on your website. You have completed four projects for Kaiser Permanente, four for Pomona College, and work for Cedars-Sinai, UCLA, Google, and The Irvine Company. Your lineage runs directly to Garrett Eckbo, one of the founders of the California modernist landscape movement. Nord Eriksson FASLA has been featured in Veranda and Martha Stewart Living.

None of this appears when a prospective client searches for you.

That is not a marketing problem. It is a structural one — and it is costing the firm real revenue.

Here is how to think about the exposure: the landscape architecture decision cycle is long. A developer planning a mixed-use project, a university planning a campus expansion, or a family commissioning an estate garden will spend 6 to 18 months researching and shortlisting firms before the first conversation. During that window, they are searching online, asking AI assistants, reading sector publications, and consulting peers. If EPTDESIGN does not appear in that research window, you are not considered. You are not rejected. You are simply never encountered.

For a firm with 35 to 40 staff and an estimated revenue of $12.7M–$50M, consider what one uncontested estate commission represents at a 20–30% profit margin, or one institutional healthcare relationship like Kaiser Permanente across four projects. The opportunity cost of invisibility is not a marketing line item. It is compounding, untracked revenue loss sustained over years.

## The Three Root Causes

The audit identified three structural causes for zero organic and AI visibility. These are not symptoms of under-investment in marketing. They are engineering and architecture decisions that are suppressing every other marketing effort the firm makes.

### Root Cause 1: AI Crawlers Are Blocked

EPTDESIGN's website currently blocks every major AI crawler via its Cloudflare configuration. This includes GPTBot (ChatGPT), ClaudeBot (Anthropic), Google-Extended (Google's AI systems), Applebot-Extended (Apple Intelligence), and others. These are the systems that read websites and decide which firms to reference when someone asks an AI assistant for a recommendation.

This block was almost certainly set automatically by Cloudflare as a default privacy measure and has never been reviewed. The practical effect is that no AI system is permitted to read EPTDESIGN's website. The firm cannot be cited by AI regardless of how strong the underlying content is. This is the highest-priority technical fix in this strategy — it can be resolved in hours.

### Root Cause 2: No Service Pages Exist

EPTDESIGN's five sectors — Residences, Commercial/Retail, Education/Healthcare, Housing/Mixed Use, and Parks/Civic — exist only as filtered views of the project portfolio at /work/. There are no dedicated pages that explain what the firm does, for whom, in which geographies, and with what depth of expertise.

Search engines and AI systems need explicit, structured content to understand what a firm offers. A filter view of project images provides none of this. When Google or an AI assistant is determining which firms to recommend for "landscape architect for healthcare campuses Los Angeles," it needs a page that makes that case — with expertise, credentials, named projects, and geographic reach. That page does not exist at EPTDESIGN.

### Root Cause 3: The Entity Is Fragmented Across the Web

"Entity" is how search engines and AI systems build a model of who an organisation is. They cross-reference NAP data (name, address, phone number), descriptions across directories, social profiles, press mentions, and backlinks to build a confidence score. When that data is inconsistent or incomplete, the confidence score is low — and the firm surfaces less often.

EPTDESIGN currently has three different Pasadena addresses in circulation. The address shown on the website does not match other listings. Google Business Profile listings across five locations show no descriptions and no services listed. The Solana Beach listing has a single 2-star review, unanswered. LinkedIn has 2,354 followers; Studio-MLA, a peer firm, has 4,663.

Each of these inconsistencies dilutes the signal Google and AI systems use to understand who EPTDESIGN is. The 64 years of work exist. The entity data that should confirm and amplify it does not.

## The Opportunity: What Sasaki's Position Represents

Sasaki is the honest benchmark for what an ambitious landscape architecture and planning firm looks like in the digital environment. Their Domain Rating of 70 means that when Google decides which firms to rank and reference, Sasaki carries significant institutional credibility. Their 88 AI citations mean they appear by name when clients ask AI assistants for recommendations across sectors and geographies.

Sasaki achieves this through four structural choices that EPTDESIGN has not yet made: dedicated sector pages that rank for specific service terms, people pages that surface principal expertise as searchable content, consistent entity data across every directory and platform, and open access for AI crawlers.

EPTDESIGN is not starting from zero. It is starting from a suppressed position — a firm with genuine authority, credentials, notable clients, and a 64-year track record that has never been properly represented in the digital environment. The gap to Sasaki is real, but the underlying assets are also real. The opportunity is to unlock them.

Byron Sharp's research on mental availability is relevant here. Buyers don't choose from all available options — they choose from the options they can readily recall when the need arises. A client entering the market for landscape architecture in Southern California will shortlist firms they have encountered in their research. Firms they have never encountered cannot be shortlisted. EPTDESIGN needs to be present — across search, across AI, across sector-relevant contexts — at the moment buyers begin looking. That is the strategic goal.

## Strategic Response: Five Priorities

The following five priorities address the root causes and build toward sustained visibility across traditional search and AI platforms. They are sequenced by impact and interdependence.

**Priority 1 — Unblock AI Crawlers (Week 1)**
A single configuration change in Cloudflare removes the blocks on GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, and related AI crawlers. This is the prerequisite for everything else. Until this is done, no content investment can reach AI platforms.

**Priority 2 — Build the Service Architecture (Months 1–3)**
Create dedicated pages for each of the five sectors, each of the five studio locations, and the firm's key credential areas (LEED, healthcare, estate, master planning). These pages carry the structured content that search engines and AI systems need to understand what EPTDESIGN offers and where.

**Priority 3 — Establish Entity Clarity (Months 1–4)**
Standardise NAP data across all directories. Complete and optimise all five Google Business Profile listings with accurate addresses, descriptions, services, and photo updates. Build or claim profiles on Houzz, Archinect, and relevant professional directories. The goal is that every data source about EPTDESIGN tells the same consistent story.

**Priority 4 — Surface the Authority Assets (Months 2–6)**
The Eckbo lineage, FASLA credentials, LEED Platinum projects, repeat institutional clients, and published press coverage in Veranda and Martha Stewart Living are real authority signals that are currently not structured as discoverable content. Principal profiles, a concise firm history with lineage context, and structured project content tied to sector pages will make this authority legible to search engines and AI systems.

**Priority 5 — Earn Third-Party Validation (Ongoing from Month 3)**
Domain Rating improves through quality backlinks — other credible websites referencing EPTDESIGN. This comes from award submissions, editorial coverage, trade publication mentions, and outreach to universities and professional associations whose websites already carry authority. The target is DR 45–50 within 12 months, which brings the firm into genuine ranking competition for its core search terms.
