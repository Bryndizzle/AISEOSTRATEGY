## 1. Where You Are Right Now — The Honest Picture

EPTDESIGN has 64 years of completed work, five studios, named relationships with Kaiser Permanente, Cedars-Sinai, Google, Pomona College, and UCLA — and zero organic search presence.

Zero is not an exaggeration. The firm currently has no measurable organic traffic, no keywords ranking in Google, and no citations in any AI system. If a healthcare system searches for landscape design firms for their new campus, EPTDESIGN does not appear. This is not a reflection of the firm's quality. It is a structural problem with three specific causes — all of which are fixable.

## 2. The Three Problems — In Plain English

### Problem 1: Your AI crawlers are switched off

Every major AI system — ChatGPT, Google's AI Overview, Perplexity, Apple Intelligence — sends automated bots to read websites and build their knowledge of the world. Those bots are currently blocked from reading the EPTDESIGN website. This is a setting in Cloudflare, the security system that sits in front of the site.

The result: when someone asks an AI to recommend a landscape architecture firm in Southern California, EPTDESIGN's website has contributed nothing to that AI's knowledge. It cannot recommend what it has never read.

**The fix:** A Cloudflare configuration change. Approximately 30 minutes. No cost. This is the single highest-priority action in this entire strategy, and it must happen before any content work begins.

### Problem 2: You have no service pages

Search engines understand what a business does by reading its pages. The EPTDESIGN website currently has no page that says "landscape architect" or "estate garden design" or "healthcare landscape architecture" or "landscape architect in Pasadena." The firm's sectors appear as filter buttons in the portfolio — but filter buttons are not pages. They are not indexed. They cannot rank.

A prospective client searching for "landscape architect Pasadena" will not find EPTDESIGN because there is no page for Google to serve them. There is no page that explains what EPTDESIGN does, who it does it for, where it operates, and why it is qualified.

**The fix:** Build the service pages that should exist. This is the majority of the content work in months 1-2 of this strategy.

### Problem 3: Your entity is fragmented

Google builds a model of every business — its name, address, what it does, who leads it, what it's known for. That model is built from dozens of sources: the website, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, ASLA directories, press mentions, and anywhere else the firm's name appears.

Currently, different sources give Google conflicting information about EPTDESIGN's Pasadena address. There are no descriptions on the Google Business Profile listings. The five locations have a combined total of five reviews. One of them — Solana Beach — has a single two-star review with no response.

Google does not have a coherent picture of who EPTDESIGN is. Until it does, the firm will not rank for anything meaningful.

**The fix:** Standardise the address across all sources, complete the GBP listings, begin accumulating reviews, and build the entity signals — the history page, the awards page, the principal bios — that tell search engines what this firm is.

## 4. The Five Priorities — In the Right Order

These must happen in sequence. Skipping to step 3 before step 1 is done is how firms spend money on content and see no results.

**Priority 1: Fix the technical blockers**
Unblock the AI crawlers. Fix the Pasadena address inconsistency. Complete the Google Business Profile listings for all five locations. Respond to the Solana Beach review. None of this requires content strategy — it requires 3-4 focused working sessions.

**Priority 2: Build the service pages that don't exist**
Launch a /services/ hub page and the three highest-priority service pages: Pasadena (location), estate residential (Nord's practice), and healthcare landscape (Stephen's practice). These pages are the foundation that everything else builds on.

**Priority 3: Win Pasadena first, then expand to Irvine and Orange County**
Dominate the local searches nearest to your headquarters before expanding. A firm that ranks clearly for Pasadena is in a far stronger position to build out Irvine, Santa Barbara, and San Diego than a firm that tries to rank everywhere simultaneously.

**Priority 4: Build the entity**
Launch the history page (1962 to now — only EPTDESIGN can write this). Build the awards consolidated page. Expand the principal bios. These pages are not marketing copy — they are the structural signals that tell search engines and AI systems who EPTDESIGN is, what it is known for, and why it should be trusted.

**Priority 5: Generate citations and authority**
Outreach to project partners (HOK, Gensler, Pomona College, UCLA) to ensure they link to EPTDESIGN where they reference shared projects. Submit to ASLA directories. Pursue press coverage, especially for Nord's residential estate work. Each citation is a data point that reinforces the entity EPTDESIGN is building.

## 5. What to Expect and When

This is not a paid advertising campaign. Organic and AI presence builds over time — but the milestones are predictable.

**Months 1-2: Foundation fixed.**
No rankings yet, and that is expected. The machine is being switched on. AI crawlers can read the site. GBP listings are complete. Service pages are live or in build. The structural problems are resolved.

**Months 3-4: First rankings appear.**
EPTDESIGN begins to appear in search results for Pasadena and specific service terms. Traffic numbers will be small — this is normal. The direction is what matters.

**Month 6: Visible in search for the first time.**
EPTDESIGN appears consistently for target searches. AI citations begin — meaning when someone asks ChatGPT or Perplexity about landscape architects in Southern California, EPTDESIGN is mentioned.

**Month 12: Established organic presence.**
Consistent organic visibility across Pasadena and the broader Southern California market. EPTDESIGN is known to search engines and AI systems as the definitive landscape architecture entity in the region. The firm has begun building a referral flywheel through citations, reviews, and partner links that compounds without ongoing spend.

There are no guarantees in organic search. There are also no shortcuts that work long-term. The strategy described here is built on entity-first thinking — communicating who EPTDESIGN is clearly and consistently, so that when a prospective client or an AI system needs to find a landscape architect in Southern California, the answer is obvious.
