# EPTDESIGN — Your 90-Day Plan

**For:** Alison Spicer, Marketing Director
**Written for:** A one-person marketing team with limited developer support
**Reading time:** 10-12 minutes — read it once, then use it as a reference

---

This plan is sequenced deliberately. The order matters. Skipping Phase 0 and going straight to content work is how firms spend money and see nothing. Do the foundation first.

---

## BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE: The 3 Non-Negotiables (Week 1)

These three actions must happen before any content work begins. They are blocking everything else.

| Action | Who | Time | Why it can't wait |
|--------|-----|------|-------------------|
| Unblock AI crawlers in Cloudflare | Your IT contact or web developer | 30 mins | Every day this stays blocked, ChatGPT, Google, Perplexity, and Apple Intelligence cannot read your website. They cannot recommend what they cannot read. |
| Respond to the Solana Beach 2-star review | Alison | 15 mins | It is the only review on that listing. Every San Diego prospect who looks up your Google listing sees a 2-star rating with no response. A professional, brief reply changes that picture immediately. |
| Fix the Pasadena address on the website | Developer | 30 mins | Three different addresses are circulating for your Pasadena office across different sources. Google cannot establish a confident picture of where you are. This is suppressing all five GBP listings, not just Pasadena. |

**How to unblock AI crawlers in Cloudflare (what to tell your IT contact or developer):**

> "We need to allow AI crawler access through Cloudflare. Specifically, we need to remove blocks on GPTBot (OpenAI), Google-Extended (Google), PerplexityBot, and AppleBot. This is in the Security > Bots settings. The robots.txt file also needs to be updated to remove any Disallow rules for these bots. It should take around 30 minutes."

**How to respond to the Solana Beach 2-star review:**

Keep it brief, professional, and specific. Do not be defensive. Example:

> "Thank you for taking the time to leave a review. We take all feedback seriously and would welcome the opportunity to understand your experience better. Please feel free to contact us directly at [email]. — The EPTDESIGN team"

## MONTH 1: Switch the Lights On

The goal this month is not rankings. It is infrastructure. By the end of Month 1, search engines should be able to find you, read you, and understand who you are.

### Week 1-2: Google Business Profile (All Five Locations)

GBP listings are the fastest-moving lever available to you. Google trusts them highly, and right now yours are essentially empty.

| Action | Owner | Effort | What it achieves |
|--------|-------|--------|-----------------|
| Write and add a 750-word description to each GBP listing | Alison (draft), principal review | 3-4 hrs total | Descriptions tell Google and prospective clients what you do and who you serve. Currently all five listings have none. |
| Add secondary categories to all listings | Alison | 1 hr | Beyond "Landscape Architect," add: Urban Design Firm, Landscape Designer, Garden Designer, Environmental Design Firm |
| Upload 10+ project photos per location | Alison | 2-3 hrs total | Photos signal activity to Google and convert casual searchers to enquiries. Use projects geographically relevant to each studio. |
| Add pre-populated Q&A to all listings | Alison | 2 hrs total | Add 3-5 questions and answers to each listing (e.g. "How long has EPTDESIGN been in Pasadena?" "What types of projects do you take on?"). Google surfaces these prominently. |
| Set up weekly GBP posts — copy from LinkedIn | Alison | 30 mins/week | GBP posts signal that the listing is active. Reuse whatever you are already posting on LinkedIn. No extra content creation needed. |

**Suggested GBP description structure (adapt for each location):**

Open with the studio's founding/location. Name the service types. Name one or two notable local projects. Name the key principal(s) at that studio. Close with a call to action. 750 words, plain English, no jargon.

## THE PRINCIPAL PITCH — For Your Conversation with Scott

You will need Scott's support before spending on developer time. Here is the ROI case in plain language.

EPTDESIGN's target clients — hospital systems, universities, major developers — do not select firms from cold calls. They research firms. That research increasingly happens on Google and through AI tools. Right now, EPTDESIGN is invisible in both.

**One institutional client relationship generates $120,000 to $350,000 or more in fees.** Often it generates multiple project phases over years. Kaiser Permanente alone represents four completed projects — a relationship that likely started with a single enquiry from a procurement team that was researching landscape architecture firms.

The question is not whether the firm can afford to do this. It is whether the firm can afford to remain invisible to the institutional clients who are researching right now.

The cost of the first three months of this programme is primarily Alison's time and developer hours. The content infrastructure built in months 2 and 3 compounds — it does not need to be rebuilt or paid for again.

This is not paid advertising. It is building a permanent structural presence that reflects the firm EPTDESIGN already is.
