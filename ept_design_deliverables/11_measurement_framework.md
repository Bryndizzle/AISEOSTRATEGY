## Section 1: Why Measurement Matters for Principal Buy-In

### Translating SEO Into Language Principals Understand

Landscape architecture principals did not train in digital marketing. They measure business success in the language of their practice: projects won, repeat clients retained, fees earned, reputation built. An SEO programme that cannot be explained in those terms will not retain principal support past the first quarter.

The table below is a translation guide. Use the right column when reporting to principals. Never use the left column with them.

| SEO term (do not use with principals) | Business outcome term (use this instead) |
|---|---|
| Organic keyword ranking | "When a developer or facilities director in Pasadena searches for a landscape architect, we now appear in the results." |
| Organic traffic | "More prospective clients are finding us through search without us paying for advertising." |
| AI citation | "When someone asks ChatGPT or Google's AI who the leading landscape architects in Southern California are, EPTDESIGN is now named." |
| Domain rating / backlinks | "Our firm's reputation and credibility online is strengthening — other respected organisations are now linking to us." |
| GBP calls | "We received [X] direct phone calls through our Google studio listings this month." |
| Impressions | "Our firm appeared in [X] searches this month — people searching for landscape architects in our markets are seeing us." |
| Click-through rate | "Of every 10 people who see us in search results, [X] are visiting our website." |
| Local pack ranking | "We appear in the top 3 map results when someone searches 'landscape architect Pasadena' on Google." |

**The core message for principals at every quarterly review:**

> "The strategy is working when: (1) our studios appear when prospective clients search for landscape architects in our cities, (2) our firm is named by AI tools when someone asks about landscape architecture in Southern California, and (3) those appearances are generating studio enquiries and RFP invitations that we can trace back to search."

### 5.1 What to Search and Where

Run this check on the first Monday of each month. Record results in the AI Citation Baseline table (Section 2.2). It takes approximately 20 minutes.

**Queries to test in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overview, and Gemini:**

| Query | Intent | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| "landscape architect pasadena ca" | Local intent | Is EPTDESIGN named? Is a competitor named instead? |
| "landscape architecture firms southern california" | Regional intent | Is EPTDESIGN in any list? |
| "Who are the leading landscape architects in Los Angeles?" | Brand/reputation | Is EPTDESIGN named alongside competitors like SWA, Rios, AECOM? |
| "landscape architect irvine california" | Local intent — secondary studio | Is EPTDESIGN named for Irvine? |
| "estate garden design pasadena" | Service + location | Does EPTDESIGN appear for estate residential? |

| "EPTDESIGN landscape architecture" | Brand query | What does each AI say about us when asked directly? |
| "Nord Eriksson landscape architect" | Named principal | Is Nord mentioned by AI? Is the attribution accurate? |

### 5.2 Using Ahrefs AI Report

[ASSUMPTION: EPTDESIGN has or will have an Ahrefs subscription. If not, manual checks are sufficient at this scale.]

Ahrefs provides an AI Visibility report (under Site Explorer > Organic Search > AI Overview) that shows:
- Which of EPTDESIGN's pages appear as sources in Google AI Overviews
- Which keywords trigger AI Overviews that EPTDESIGN could appear in
- Whether competitors are cited instead

Review this report monthly alongside the manual checks above.

### 5.3 What to Look For

When checking AI citation results, note:

- **Are we mentioned?** (Yes / No)
- **Are we linked?** (Some AI tools link to sources; others name them without linking)
- **What are we cited for?** (If cited, note the context — location, service type, era/history)
- **Who is cited instead?** (Note competitors who appear — this tells you what to build toward)
- **Is the information accurate?** (AI occasionally misattributes information — see Section 5.4)

### 5.4 How to Respond If Cited Incorrectly

AI systems sometimes generate inaccurate information about businesses — wrong founding date, incorrect project attribution, wrong principals named, wrong locations.

**If you discover an incorrect citation:**

1. **Do not panic.** AI citation errors are common and are not permanent.
2. **Document it.** Screenshot the incorrect citation, noting the platform, query, and exact text.
3. **Strengthen the correct information on your website.** The primary way to correct AI misattribution is to make the correct information more prominent, well-structured, and clearly marked with schema on EPTDESIGN's own pages.
4. **For Google AI Overview specifically:** If the error appears in a Google AI Overview, you can use the thumbs-down / feedback mechanism to flag it. This does not guarantee immediate correction but flags it to Google.
5. **For ChatGPT / Perplexity:** These systems update as they re-crawl the web. Improving on-site structured data (schema) and building more authoritative citations from reliable third-party sources (ASLA, Wikipedia if applicable, Archinect, trade press) will help correct the AI's understanding over time.
6. **For serious misattribution** (e.g. a completed project attributed to a competitor, or a factual error about the firm's founding): escalate to Scott Horsley. Consider whether a targeted PR piece or press release is warranted to place the correct information on authoritative third-party sites.
